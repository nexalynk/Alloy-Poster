How to Prepare Your Photo for a Metal Print

Published 2026-10-19

Get a sharper, truer print: use the original file, crop to the right shape, and adjust brightness and colour before you upload.

Use the original file

Photos saved from messaging apps and social media are shrunk and compressed. Upload the original from your camera or phone, and avoid screenshots.

Crop to the print's shape

Sizes such as 8 × 10 and 16 × 20 are 4:5, while 12 × 18, 20 × 30 and 24 × 36 are 2:3. A mismatch means part of the photo is trimmed. Drag and zoom in the customizer to choose what stays in the frame.

Mind brightness and colour

Screens glow, so a photo that looks perfect on your phone can print darker. If a photo is dim, brighten it slightly before uploading. Our Auto-enhance option adds a gentle boost to contrast and colour.

Skip heavy filters and sharpening

Strong sharpening and filters can look harsh at large sizes. A clean, natural photo usually prints best.
