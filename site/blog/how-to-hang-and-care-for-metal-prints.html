How to Hang and Care for a Metal Print

Published 2026-10-19

Hanging height, mounting options and simple care tips to keep a metal print looking new.

Choose a mounting style

Alloy Poster offers Standard, Floating and Magnetic mounting. Pick the one that suits your wall and how often you like to change your art. Check the product page for the current options.

Hang it well

Mark the centre at about 57 inches (145 cm) from the floor, use a level, and use wall anchors that suit your wall type for larger sizes. Ask a second person to help with big prints.

Keep it clean

Dust with a dry microfibre cloth. For fingerprints, use a slightly damp soft cloth and dry it straight away. Avoid abrasive pads and harsh chemical cleaners.

Placement tips

Choose a spot away from steam and constant direct sunlight, and a matte finish if the wall faces a bright window.
