How to Choose the Right Size for Your Metal Print

Published 2026-10-19

Simple rules for choosing a print size: wall space, furniture, hanging height and the resolution your photo needs.

Start with the wall

Over a sofa or bed, aim for a print about two-thirds the width of the furniture. A single large print feels more intentional than several small ones scattered along a wall.

Hang it at eye level

Galleries usually hang art so the centre sits about 57 inches (145 cm) from the floor. Over furniture, leave roughly 6 to 8 inches (15 to 20 cm) between the bottom of the print and the top of the piece.

Check your photo's resolution

For a sharp print, aim for about 150 pixels per inch at the size you choose. A 3000 × 4500 pixel photo suits roughly 20 × 30 inches. Our customizer shows an estimate and a quality rating as soon as you upload.
