Custom Metal Print Gift Ideas for Canadian Occasions

Published 2026-10-19

Gift ideas for Mother's Day, Father's Day, Canada Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas, weddings and new homes, plus how early to order.

Family occasions

Mother's Day (second Sunday of May) and Father's Day (third Sunday of June) suit a favourite family portrait or a photo of the kids together. Anniversaries and weddings are a good fit for a print of the venue or a favourite moment from the day.

Seasonal gifts

Thanksgiving (second Monday of October) and Christmas are good moments for a group photo from a family gathering. Canada Day, July 1, is a nice excuse to print a favourite trip, from the coast to the Rockies to a quiet lake.

Pets and new homes

A pet portrait is a popular choice, and a new-home print gives a bare wall a personal touch. Children's artwork also turns into a keepsake.

Order early

Each print is made to order. Allow 3 to 5 business days for production plus shipping time, and order well ahead of holidays.
