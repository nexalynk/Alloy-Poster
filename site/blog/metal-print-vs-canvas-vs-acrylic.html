Metal Print vs. Canvas vs. Acrylic: Which to Choose?

Published 2026-10-19

A plain-language comparison of metal prints, canvas and acrylic for photos and art: look, durability, weight and cost.

Metal prints

The image is printed onto aluminum. Fine detail stays crisp, colours look vivid and the smooth surface gives a clean, modern, frameless look. Metal is light for its size and wipes clean with a soft cloth.

Canvas

Canvas is printed fabric stretched over a wooden frame. The woven texture gives a soft, painterly feel that suits landscapes and traditional rooms, but it hides fine detail and can be dented if knocked.

Acrylic

Acrylic prints put the image behind or on a sheet of clear acrylic for a deep, glossy look. They can scratch more easily than metal, and large sizes get heavy. They are often priced higher.

Which should you pick?

Choose metal for sharp photos, bold colour and a contemporary space. Choose canvas for a softer, cosier look. In a bright room with lots of windows, a matte finish cuts glare whichever material you pick.
