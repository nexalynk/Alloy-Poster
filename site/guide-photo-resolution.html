Photo resolution guide

How big can you print?

Rule of thumb

Aim for about 150 pixels per inch. A 3000 × 4500 px photo suits roughly 20 × 30 in.

Phone photos

Recent phone photos usually work well up to 16 × 20 in. Avoid screenshots and messaging-app copies.

Check before you buy

The customizer shows an estimated PPI and quality rating for your chosen size.
